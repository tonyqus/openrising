## How could monthly savings of roughly CNY9,000 in London arise?

The earlier discussion raised this possibility. The reconstructed teaching scenario below makes its assumptions visible. It is not a set of actual quotes, a current exchange rate or a verified household budget.

| Item | Monthly amount (GBP) | Basis |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Take-home income | 4,088 | Historical discussion figure above |
| Rent | 1,700 | Historical discussion figure above |
| Food | 350 | Teaching assumption |
| Transport | 200 | Teaching assumption |
| Utilities and communications | 200 | Teaching assumption |
| Other necessary and miscellaneous expenses | 250 | Teaching assumption |
| Monthly allocation for annual costs | 388 | Teaching assumption |
| Total expenses | 3,088 | Sum of expenses above |
| Monthly surplus | 1,000 | Income minus total expenses |

**For illustration only**, at GBP1 = CNY9, GBP1,000 becomes CNY9,000. This is not a current exchange-rate quotation.

Using the same illustrative exchange rate:

| Scenario | Take-home income (GBP) | Expenses (GBP) | Monthly surplus (GBP) | Equivalent CNY |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Baseline assumptions | 4,088 | 3,088 | 1,000 | 9,000 |
| Income falls 20% | 3,270.4 | 3,088 | 182.4 | 1,641.6 |
| Expenses rise 20% | 4,088 | 3,705.6 | 382.4 | 3,441.6 |
| Both changes occur | 3,270.4 | 3,705.6 | -435.2 | -3,916.8 |

Roughly CNY9,000 is therefore a scenario result tied to particular income, housing, lifestyle and exchange-rate assumptions. It does not establish that London generally offers better savings than Shanghai. Calculate an equivalent Shanghai budget using obtainable pay and complete expenses before comparing.

## Sources and verification

- [Numbeo: Shanghai](https://www.numbeo.com/cost-of-living/in/Shanghai) and [London](https://www.numbeo.com/cost-of-living/in/London): dynamic pages. Record access dates, currency, housing type and sample notes. Corresponding snapshots for the historical values above are missing.
- [Expatistan](https://www.expatistan.com/cost-of-living): verify housing coverage, household size and consumption basket before combining figures with other sources.
- [Digital nomad analysis reference project](https://github.com/Aventardo7777/digital-nomad-data-analysis): its README describes a course assignment and simulated collection. It is a learning reference; the charts are not certified here as observed market results.
- Employer vacancies and actual housing listings: collect fresh evidence for personal decisions. Retain URLs, dates, original currencies, target populations, experience requirements and location restrictions.
